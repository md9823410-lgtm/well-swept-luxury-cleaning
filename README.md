**Design & Branding**
Create a high-end luxury editorial website using sophisticated hot pink, blush pink, ivory, champagne and elegant dark accents. Use premium typography, stunning cleaning photography, refined glass effects, smooth animations, beautiful layouts and sophisticated micro-interactions. Avoid generic templates and unnecessary content.

**Hero Banner**
Design an extraordinary full-width luxury hero banner inspired by the provided flyer. Make it visually breathtaking, expensive and unique—not like a basic flyer. Feature premium cleaning photography, elegant pink visual accents, beautiful typography and a strong composition. Headline: “More Than Cleaning, It’s Peace of Mind.” Highlight “Bundle Deals & Exclusive Discounts” with an elegant promotional design. Include a prominent Call/Text Britney button and phone number. Ensure perfect desktop and mobile responsiveness.

**Only These Website Sections**

**Integrate Both Supplied Components**
- Save `3d-carousel.tsx` in `/components/ui/` and integrate `ThreeDPhotoCarousel` into the Interactive 3D Cleaning Gallery. Replace all random city images with relevant premium residential and commercial cleaning photos.
- Save `glassy-coverflow-carousel.tsx` in `/components/ui/` and integrate its default `GlassyCarousel` export into the Glassy Coverflow Cleaning Showcase. Replace all movie posters, movie names, trailers and demo branding with relevant cleaning project images and editable cleaning content. Use `layout="poster"` and a beautiful pink, blush and ivory luxury theme. Set `autoPlay={false}`.

**Technical Requirements**
Use React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui. Install `framer-motion` and `lucide-react`. Preserve both components' original functionality. Make both carousels responsive, interactive and visually polished. Use working image URLs and relevant premium cleaning photography.

**Important**
Do not add FAQ, process, blog, or any unnecessary sections. Do not invent customer reviews or business claims. Keep all business information, images, offers and testimonials editable. Maintain consistent luxury styling across every section. Preserve existing working features. Deliver a complete, polished, production-ready website.
